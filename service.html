AI

AI Services

Agentic tools development

We build AI agents that can plan, use tools and complete multi-step tasks, such as reading documents, querying your systems, updating records and handing off to people when needed. Each agent is designed around your process, with clear guardrails and a log of every action it takes.

Custom AI workflow development

We map your existing processes and identify where AI adds real value, then build workflows that connect language models with your apps, data and APIs. These include support triage, report generation, document processing and internal knowledge assistants, built to fit how your team already works.

Model fine-tuning

When a general-purpose model isn't enough, we fine-tune models on your own data so they understand your domain, terminology and tone. We handle data preparation, training, evaluation and deployment, and we compare results against the base model so you can see the improvement.

AI assistance & consulting

Not sure where to start? We help you choose the right models and tools, run proofs of concept, estimate costs and plan a roll-out. After launch, we monitor, refine and extend your AI workflows as your needs grow.
